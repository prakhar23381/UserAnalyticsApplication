About Acme Roasters

Three people, one roaster and a lot of tasting spoons.

Our story

Acme started in a garage with a second-hand two-kilo roaster and a simple rule: never sell a coffee we wouldn't drink every morning ourselves. Ten years later the roaster is bigger, the garage is a warehouse, and the rule hasn't changed.

We roast four days a week. Orders that come in before noon are roasted the next morning and shipped that afternoon, so the coffee reaches you while it is still at its best.

The roastery

How we roast

Every coffee gets its own roast profile. We roast a sample batch, taste it on three different brewers, adjust, and repeat until the coffee tastes like the farm it came from rather than like the roast.

Lighter roasts keep the fruit and florals of washed African coffees. Our espresso is roasted longer for body and sweetness in milk drinks, but never to the point where it turns bitter.

Cupping table

Where our coffee comes from

We buy directly from eleven farms and two cooperatives, most of them for five years or more. We pay above Fair Trade minimums and publish what we pay per kilo on every product page.

Long relationships let farmers plan ahead: they know what we will buy next year, and we can work with them on new processing experiments instead of chasing whatever is fashionable.
